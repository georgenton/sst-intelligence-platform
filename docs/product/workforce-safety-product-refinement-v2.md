# Workforce Safety Product Refinement V2

Status: implementation candidate on `feat/workforce-safety-product-refinement-v2`; external audit
and merge are still required.

## DONE on the candidate branch

- Position and structured Position risk context integrated with Worker, Work Center and Work Area.
- Deterministic risk-to-EPP candidates, professional Position requirement, Worker assignment,
  historical replacement and explicit Incident link.
- Accidentes e Incidentes location, attention priority, investigation method and EPP traceability.
- Authenticated Safety Observation lifecycle, evidence, existing-Action link and Work Queue item.
- Training Need provenance, finite audiences, linked sessions and printable plan.
- Canonical Work Queue projections; no duplicated execution lifecycle.
- Additive migration, tenant validation and regression coverage.

## SYNTHETIC

- Risk-category-to-EPP candidate mappings and electrical-maintenance demonstration examples.
- Demo wording, example Position/Worker/events, reference metadata and handoff scenarios.
- These artifacts are operational test/reference material, not legal, certification or Anita-approved
  content.

## PENDING ANITA

- Position/risk/EPP terminology and which candidate mappings should be retained.
- Standard and certification references and the evidence required for professional confirmation.
- Accident/Incident location terms, investigation factor labels and Ishikawa fit.
- Training-need triggers, delivery/certification classification and management-plan content.
- One-minute Safety Observation intake and attention criteria.

## DEFERRED

- Anonymous/public QR reporting, deep mobile/PWA and offline operation.
- Notification providers, external Workforce AI and production OpenAI.
- Psychosocial, occupational-health clinical records and medical decisions.
- Stock/procurement/ERP, a complete LMS, electronic-signature provider and legal filing automation.

## Permanent boundaries

Worker is not User or Membership. Plan Operativo is not Work Queue. Safety Observation is not
Inspection, Finding or Incident. EPP Requirement is not an Issue. Training Need is not a Session or
Completion. Deterministic suggestion is not a professional decision. The API and current
organization Membership remain authoritative.
