# Roadmap modular

El incremento actual entrega CORE y presentaciones conceptuales de INSPECTIONS_INTELLIGENCE,
TECHNICAL_RISK, WORK_PERMITS, PSYCHOSOCIAL y COMPLIANCE. Los siguientes slices deben desarrollar un
módulo por vez, incluyendo modelo aislado por `organizationId`, permisos, entitlements, auditoría,
pruebas y experiencia completa. Salud ocupacional, pagos, archivos, OCR, RAG y aplicación móvil no
están autorizados en esta etapa.

## Risk Methodology Engine

Phase 1 defines technical-source, method-version, provider, expert-guidance and regulatory-context
contracts without connecting them to production runtime. GTC45 and GUIDED_5X5 remain DEMO/CANDIDATE
with technical and legal review pending. Product Walkthrough Hardening V1.1 completed the auth,
revision, verification, recurrence, Work Center and presentation seams required before Prisma, API,
seed, Inspection and method-selection runtime integration.

Historical `DEMO_5X5@1.0.0` remains unchanged. No future method may recalculate existing findings,
and initial/residual valuation must retain the same exact method version.
