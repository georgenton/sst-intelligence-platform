# Method versus regulatory context

## Non-equivalence

```text
Regulatory source ≠ methodology source ≠ risk method ≠ expert guidance ≠ assessment
```

LAW/REGULATION tells us what obligations and context to consider. METHODOLOGY tells us how a risk
is evaluated. EXPERT GUIDANCE helps a professional apply the methodology.

Linking a method to a jurisdiction explains where it may be considered; it does not turn the method
into law.

## Current Ecuador evidence

The following official Ministry sources were checked on 2026-08-25:

- Decreto Ejecutivo 255, article 47: recognized methodologies must be applied to identify and
  evaluate risk situations. Official PDF:
  https://www.trabajo.gob.ec/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/DECRETO-EJECUTIVO-255-REGLAMENTO-DE-SEGURIDAD-Y-SALUD-DE-LOS-TRABAJADORES.pdf
- Anexo 1 — Lista de Verificación SST: asks whether the job-position matrix applies a nationally or
  internationally recognized and validated methodology, and separately asks for residual-risk
  calculation. Official PDF:
  https://www.trabajo.gob.ec/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Anexo-1_Lista-de-Verificacion-SST-signed-signed_08042025.pdf
- Official normative index:
  https://www.trabajo.gob.ec/normativa-legal-programas-formatos-y-guias/

These records support a general recognized-method principle. They do not establish that Ecuador
officially endorses GTC45 or this product's GUIDED_5X5 candidate.

The source identities already exist as `EC_EXECUTIVE_DECREE_255` and
`EC_MDT_2024_196_ANNEX_1`. Runtime context records reference them editorially without adding
provisions, requirements or rules.

## Risk classifications and residual matrix

Decree 255 provides occupational-risk classifications and the verification annex expects residual
risk to be calculated. Those facts are context requirements, not a formula selection. The same
method version must be used for initial and residual revaluation unless a future reviewed rule says
otherwise.

## GTC45 context

GTC45 is a Colombian technical-method candidate. Its Ecuador context starts with
`TECHNICAL_REVIEW_PENDING` and `LEGAL_REVIEW_PENDING`. It must not be labeled
`ECUADOR_LEGAL_METHOD` or `APPROVED_FOR_PRODUCTION`.
